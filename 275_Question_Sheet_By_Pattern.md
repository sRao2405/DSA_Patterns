## Quick takeaways

- **Trees & Graphs (DFS/BFS/Topo/Union Find)** and **DP** together make up close to half the sheet — this matches what we said earlier: budget the most weeks there.
- **Hashing/Prefix Sum** is a huge hidden bucket — worth treating as its own mini-pattern even though it's not in your original 20, since ~35+ problems lean on it.
- **Monotonic Stack** and **Modified Binary Search** are smaller but high-frequency-in-interviews categories — good ROI per problem solved.
- Hards are concentrated in a few specific areas (DP, Binary Search on Answer, Hard backtracking, KMP) — you can safely defer almost all of them to a later revision pass without losing coverage of the core 20 patterns.
